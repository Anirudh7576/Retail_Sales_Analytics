1. Project Overview

  ABC Retail Ltd. is a global retail company that sells consumer products through online platforms and physical stores. The company operates across multiple countries and receives millions of sales transactions from its website, mobile application, and retail outlets. To support business growth and data-driven decision-making, the company requires a modern cloud-based ELT platform that can process large volumes of data efficiently and provide reliable analytics.

2. Business Problem
  
  a) Reports are delayed.
  b) Manual effort is high.
  c) Data is inconsistent.
  d) Different departments see different sales numbers.
  e) Historical data is difficult to analyze.
  f) Business users spend significant time manually preparing reports.

3. Business Objectives

  a) Deliver faster and reliable business reports.
  b) Improve dashboard performance for business users.
  c) Reduce infrastructure and operational costs.
  d) Improve data quality and consistency across all business reports.
  e) Enable near real-time business insights.
